Official Incident Report

Unauthorised Contractor Investigation

A security investigation involving an unidentified male, a staff vehicle and extensive woof-based reporting.

Incident Summary

An unidentified male was observed outside company headquarters conducting unexplained activity involving a staff vehicle. The individual had not been introduced to the CEO. No prior security briefing had been provided. Management therefore initiated an immediate investigation.

Case File #5189

Department
Security
Priority
Elevated
Lead Investigator
CEO Enzo

Timeline of Events

  • Contractor Detected CEO Enzo became aware of an unfamiliar male operating in the vicinity of company headquarters.
  • Security Patrol Initiated Management began moving repeatedly between the front door and kitchen while issuing low-level verbal warnings.
  • Primary Hooman Interviewed The Primary Hooman attempted to establish the nature of the security concern using a series of investigative prompts including: "What is it?" "What do you hear?" "Tell me more." CEO provided a comprehensive verbal report.
  • Surveillance Continues Despite reassurances from staff, management determined that the situation required further monitoring. Woofing continued.
  • Identity Confirmed The suspicious individual was eventually identified as a legitimate contractor who had been invited to programme a replacement electronic key for a staff vehicle. Threat level was subsequently downgraded.

Evidence Collected

  • One unidentified male outside headquarters.
  • One staff vehicle receiving unexplained attention.
  • Repeated CEO security patrols.
  • Multiple verbal warnings.
  • Detailed witness statement provided entirely in woofs.
  • Video evidence documenting the investigation.
  • Subsequent confirmation of legitimate contractor status.
  • No evidence that staff would have survived the incident without CEO supervision.

Investigation Findings

Following a thorough review, investigators determined that the individual did not represent a genuine security threat. The contractor had been legitimately engaged by staff to programme a replacement electronic key for a recently acquired vehicle.

However, management identified a serious procedural failure. The Security Department had not been informed in advance.

The CEO was therefore confronted with an unknown individual apparently interfering with company property and was forced to initiate emergency surveillance without adequate briefing. Investigators concluded that Enzo's response was proportionate to the information available to him.

Security Assessment

  • Contractor did not attempt unauthorised entry.
  • Contractor did not remove company property.
  • Assigned work was successfully completed.
  • Contractor was apparently known to the hoomans.
  • Credentials were not provided directly to the CEO.

The final point remains under review.

Official Case Outcome

🟢 Contractor Cleared — Staff Warning Issued

No evidence of malicious activity was discovered. The contractor has been removed from the list of active security threats. Staff, however, have been formally reminded that all unfamiliar visitors, contractors, delivery personnel and individuals behaving suspiciously near company property should be declared to the Security Department in advance. Failure to do so may result in further woof-based investigations.
